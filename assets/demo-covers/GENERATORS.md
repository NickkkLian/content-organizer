# Demo cover generators

All covers are original SVG drawings generated by `tools/build-demo.mjs` and licensed under the repository MIT licence. Temple roofs identify Kyoto notes, route diagrams identify travel videos, plates identify food, key matrices identify desk notes, lamps identify home notes and open books identify mixed practical notes. There are no faces, logos or creator imitations. The guide retains the east-bank route diagram because its path and closed fork need a spatial view. The other diagrams repeat facts kept in the text and are omitted from the guide. This build uses the SVG fallback approved in BRIEF.md.
